
# VentureMind

### Deep Research & Entrepreneurial Intelligence for Google Antigravity

**Think deeper. Research harder. Validate before you build.**

VentureMind is an evidence-driven research skill for Google Antigravity that helps founders, developers, entrepreneurs and product builders make better decisions before investing time and money.

It investigates business ideas, existing products, customer complaints, market demand, competitors, search trends, pricing, technical feasibility and business risks.

## What Can VentureMind Do?

| Research Engine | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Deep Research | Investigates topics using multiple sources |
| Market Intelligence | Evaluates market demand and competitors |
| Customer Intelligence | Analyzes reviews, complaints and customer needs |
| Entrepreneurial Thinking | Evaluates opportunities using founder-level thinking |
| SEO Intelligence | Investigates keywords and search demand |
| Marketing Intelligence | Evaluates positioning and distribution |
| Product Intelligence | Studies features, gaps and differentiation |
| Business Intelligence | Evaluates revenue models and financial feasibility |
| Domain Intelligence | Researches domain availability, pricing and branding |
| Technical Risk | Evaluates implementation complexity and risks |
| Red-Team Verification | Challenges assumptions and verifies findings |
| Execution Strategy | Recommends practical next steps |

## Research Capabilities

- Deep web research and evidence verification.
- Competitor discovery and product comparisons.
- Reddit, Google Play Store and Apple App Store review analysis where accessible.
- Customer pain-point identification.
- Google Trends and keyword demand research.
- SEO and ASO opportunity analysis.
- Pricing and willingness-to-pay investigation.
- Business model and financial feasibility.
- Domain availability and brand conflict research.
- Technical, legal and operational risk assessment.
- Evidence-based opportunity scoring.
- Professional PDF research reports.

## Installation

Clone the repository:

```bash
git clone https://github.com/gleamsolsolutions/venturemind.git
```

Install globally:

```bash
mkdir -p ~/.gemini/config/skills
cp -R venturemind ~/.gemini/config/skills/
```

Restart or reload Antigravity if necessary.

## How to Use

Invoke the skill:

```text
/venturemind
```

Example research requests:

```text
/venturemind Evaluate this Android app idea for a global paid market.

/venturemind Research whether this SaaS opportunity is worth building.

/venturemind Find underserved customer problems in this market.

/venturemind Analyze competitors, customer complaints and pricing.

/venturemind Identify profitable product opportunities for developers.
```

## Research Philosophy

VentureMind follows an evidence-first approach:

1. Investigate before making recommendations.
2. Verify important claims using independent sources.
3. Identify existing competitors and explain differentiation.
4. Separate verified facts from assumptions.
5. Challenge weak business models and unsupported demand.
6. Recheck important findings before reaching conclusions.
7. Recommend practical experiments before major investment.

## Final Verdict

Research concludes with one of five recommendations:

- Strong Opportunity
- Opportunity with Differentiation
- Needs Validation
- Weak Opportunity
- Do Not Pursue

## Important Limitations

VentureMind is a research and decision-support skill, not an independent data provider.

Research coverage depends on available browser access, search engines, APIs, specialist tools and source accessibility. It does not bypass private groups, authentication, paywalls or restricted platforms.

It must not invent search volumes, customer feedback, domain availability or financial metrics.
