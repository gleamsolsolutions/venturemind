# Market Intelligence Engine

Map market category, customer segments, direct/indirect competitors, substitutes, DIY workarounds and do-nothing. Estimate market size bottom-up where possible using explicit segment counts, reachable share and realistic spend. Avoid multiplying an unsupported global population by an arbitrary price. Track market timing, regulation, category growth/decline, entrants, exits and failed products. Separate total market from reachable market and obtainable share.
