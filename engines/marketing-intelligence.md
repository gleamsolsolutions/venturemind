# Marketing Intelligence Engine

Define ideal customer, job, trigger, desired outcome, objections and alternatives. Develop positioning, value proposition, messaging, trust signals and brand narrative. Map channels to where customers already seek help. Design an ethical acquisition loop, referral reason, activation path and retention mechanism. Explain why a customer notices, tries, trusts, returns to and recommends the product. Quantify channel assumptions only with evidence or explicit hypotheses. Avoid vague claims of virality.
