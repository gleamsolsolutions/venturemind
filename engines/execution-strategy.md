# Execution and Experimentation Engine

Read `references/experimentation-playbook.md`. Recommend validation tests before major build spend. Define hypothesis, segment, method, sample rationale, cost, duration, metrics, guardrails, success/failure thresholds and decision rule. Build a staged roadmap with decision gates, owners only if known, dependencies and measurable outcomes. Include first 10, 100 and 1,000 customer acquisition plans. Do not treat a roadmap as a commitment or assume unvalidated forecasts are targets.
