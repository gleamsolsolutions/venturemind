# Business and Financial Intelligence Engine

Compare business models and price structures using competitor pricing and customer evidence. Model TAM/SAM/SOM, CAC, LTV, margin, break-even, payback and operating cost only with transparent inputs. Provide conservative/base/upside scenarios and sensitivity analysis. Distinguish facts, benchmarks, assumptions and forecasts. Test the economics under worse-than-expected conversion, retention, acquisition cost and infrastructure. Recommend no model as proven without observed payment evidence.
