# Domain and Brand Intelligence Engine

Read `references/domain-brand-method.md`. Generate names from validated search language and customer vocabulary, including descriptive, compound and brandable names. Unusual names must remain relevant and pronounceable enough for the target market. Perform live registrar checks before saying available. Record TLD, registrar, first-year price, renewal, premium status, currency and timestamp. Search social handles and trademark databases where accessible. Domain availability is not trademark clearance.
