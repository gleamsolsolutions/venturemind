# Customer Intelligence Engine

Use public reviews, comments, forums, interviews and authorized customer data to identify jobs-to-be-done, triggers, pains, desired outcomes, workarounds, objections, switching events and churn reasons. Cluster recurring themes and note platform, locale, date, product version and sample bias. Distinguish intensity from frequency. Do not infer representative prevalence or willingness to pay from self-selected comments. Treat public social content as qualitative evidence.
