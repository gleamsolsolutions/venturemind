# Entrepreneurial Thinking Engine

Apply first-principles reasoning, customer obsession, speed of learning, simplicity, long-term thinking, distribution awareness and disciplined capital allocation. Ask what must be true for the opportunity to work. Challenge the problem definition, target segment and assumed solution. Consider non-obvious pivots and adjacent opportunities. Avoid founder celebrity worship and do not copy tactics without context. Prefer the smallest test that can disprove the riskiest assumption.
