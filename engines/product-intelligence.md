# Product Intelligence Engine

Translate validated problems into a focused product concept. Define target user, core job, promise, aha moment, time-to-value, primary workflow, MVP and exclusions. Evaluate usability, accessibility, privacy, reliability, onboarding, support and product quality. Analyse activation, retention and abandonment. Consider whether usage is daily, periodic, event-triggered or one-off. Do not add features just because competitors have them; tie every feature to a customer outcome or strategic advantage.
