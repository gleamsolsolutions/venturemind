# VentureMind Opportunity Scorecard

Score each dimension from 0 to 5. Multiply the score/5 by its weight. Total is out of 100.

| Dimension | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Verified customer demand | 15 |
| Problem severity/frequency | 10 |
| Market opportunity | 10 |
| Competition and differentiation | 15 |
| Revenue potential | 10 |
| Customer acquisition/distribution | 10 |
| Retention and repeat value | 10 |
| Technical feasibility | 5 |
| Financial viability | 5 |
| Long-term defensibility | 5 |
| Timing | 5 |
| **Total** | **100** |

## Anchors
- 0: Evidence indicates absent or prohibitive.
- 1: Very weak; substantial adverse evidence.
- 2: Weak or mostly unverified.
- 3: Plausible, mixed or partially validated.
- 4: Strong supporting evidence with manageable uncertainty.
- 5: Strong, independently corroborated evidence.

Unknown is not a 3. Mark it **N/A / unknown**, explain why, and show a provisional score range rather than inventing a midpoint. If a weighted total is calculated without an unknown dimension, normalize only across scored weights and label it provisional.

## Confidence
- High: critical claims are supported by multiple independent credible sources or primary evidence.
- Medium: meaningful evidence exists, but gaps or conflicting findings remain.
- Low: sparse, stale, indirect or weak evidence.

Confidence is separate from attractiveness. A high score with low confidence means “promising but unproven”, not “go build”.

## Hard-stop overrides
A legal, safety, platform-policy, technical, privacy or unit-economics blocker can override a high numerical score. Explain the blocker and conditions for reconsideration.

## Decision bands (guidance, not automatic truth)
- 80–100: Strong opportunity, if no hard-stop and confidence is at least medium.
- 65–79: Opportunity with differentiation; run focused validation.
- 45–64: Needs validation or repositioning.
- Below 45: Weak opportunity; usually do not pursue.
Do not let the score replace judgement. A strong counterargument can change the verdict.
