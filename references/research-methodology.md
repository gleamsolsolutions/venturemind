# Research Methodology

## Query expansion
Build query families:
- Exact product/idea phrase.
- Synonyms and category names.
- Customer problem phrasing.
- Alternative and competitor names.
- “alternative to”, “vs”, “best”, “review”, “complaint”, “problem”, “missing feature”, “refund”, “cancel”, “too expensive”, “doesn't work”, “workaround”.
- Pricing, terms, policies, API limits and technical constraints.
- Failure, shutdown, acquisition, pivot and discontinued product.
- Country/language-specific phrases.

## Source coverage matrix
Select relevant sources, not every source mechanically:
- Search engines and official websites.
- Google Trends and keyword tools.
- Google Play and Apple App Store.
- Reddit and public forums.
- Public Facebook posts/groups only where accessible and permitted.
- YouTube comments and product demos.
- GitHub issues/discussions/releases.
- Product Hunt.
- G2, Capterra, Trustpilot and other review platforms.
- Industry publications, public filings and government/regulator sources.
- Domain registrars, WHOIS/RDAP and trademark databases.

Record unavailable sources. Never imply exhaustive coverage of “the entire web”.

## Stop rule
Stop when:
- The original decision criteria have been addressed.
- Critical claims have traceable evidence or are explicitly marked unknown.
- Important counterarguments have been investigated.
- New searches are returning repeated evidence with diminishing likelihood of changing the decision.
- The report states remaining uncertainty and next validation steps.

Do not stop merely after a fixed number of searches or sources.
