# SEO, ASO and Search Demand Playbook

## Search demand
For every keyword record: exact query, language, country, date range, source/tool, retrieval date, volume unit and whether volume is exact, rounded, modelled or unavailable.

Google Trends provides relative normalized interest, not absolute search volume. Do not interpret an index of 100 as 100 searches. Compare terms in consistent geography and time windows.

Google Keyword Planner values can be ranges or account-dependent estimates. Bing tools measure their own ecosystem. Do not relabel Bing estimates as Yahoo-specific data. Yahoo search data should be reported only if a source explicitly provides Yahoo-specific measurement.

## Intent classification
- Informational: learning or solving a question.
- Navigational: finding a brand/site/app.
- Commercial investigation: comparing options.
- Transactional: ready to buy, subscribe, download or hire.
- Local: location-dependent service or discovery.

## Keyword clusters
Cluster by customer job, stage and intent. Include:
- Core category terms.
- Problem terms.
- Solution terms.
- Alternative/comparison terms.
- Feature terms.
- Pricing and purchase terms.
- Long-tail questions.
- Competitor and brand terms.

Do not recommend targeting a keyword solely because it has high volume. Assess SERP intent, competition, content quality, authority gap and realistic ranking feasibility.

## SEO analysis
Inspect actual result pages and record date, location, device if relevant, result type, dominant intent, ranking domains, content format, freshness and apparent barriers. Use SEO tool metrics only with tool name and retrieval date.

## ASO
For apps inspect store title, subtitle/short description, long description, category, reviews, screenshots, icon, update history, price, in-app purchases, localization and current policy. Do not infer exact install volume unless the store/tool exposes it reliably.

## Deliverable
Provide prioritized keyword clusters with intent, evidence of demand, competition notes, content/product implications and confidence. Separate observed metrics from qualitative SERP observations.
