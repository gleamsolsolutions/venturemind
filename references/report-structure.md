# Report Structure and Content Rules

Adapt the structure to the question. Avoid filler and irrelevant chapters.

1. Cover
2. Executive summary
3. Direct answer and final verdict
4. Scope, assumptions and research cutoff
5. Market landscape and existing solutions
6. Customer evidence and recurring pain points
7. Search demand, Trends, SEO/ASO
8. Competitor comparison and differentiation
9. Opportunity discovery and product strategy
10. Positioning, marketing and distribution
11. Business model and financial scenarios
12. Domain and brand shortlist
13. Technical, operational, legal and platform risks
14. Red-team challenge and resolution
15. Validation experiments and execution roadmap
16. Scorecard and confidence
17. Methodology, limitations and sources

For a narrow question, omit irrelevant sections. For a general subject question, lead with the answer and include only relevant analysis. Always make the conclusion understandable without reading every appendix.
