# Domain and Brand Research

## Naming workflow
1. Extract validated customer language and keywords.
2. Create descriptive, compound, benefit-led, brandable and coined name candidates.
3. Include unconventional names only if memorable, pronounceable enough and meaningfully linked to the customer job, keyword family or positioning.
4. Screen spelling, pronunciation, ambiguity, negative meanings and expansion constraints.
5. Check domain availability and pricing at a registrar/registry.
6. Check renewal pricing, premium status, taxes and restrictions.
7. Check social handles where possible.
8. Search trademark databases and confusingly similar brands in relevant jurisdictions.

## Default budget
Prefer standard registrations below USD 20/year where feasible. This is a filter, not a promise. Premium domains are excluded by default. Show first-year and renewal prices separately. Convert currencies only with a dated exchange rate and cite the rate source.

## Availability labels
- Verified available: live registrar/registry lookup performed at stated time.
- Registered: lookup confirms already registered.
- Premium: registrar labels as premium; report price and renewal if available.
- Unverified candidate: no authoritative live lookup; do not call available.
- Restricted/unsupported: TLD or registration requirements need further review.

A domain search result, DNS response, or absence of a website does not prove availability. Domain availability is not trademark clearance. Recommend qualified trademark review before material investment.
